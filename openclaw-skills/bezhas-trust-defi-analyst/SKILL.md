# BeZhas Trust & DeFi Analyst

Analyze financial utility without promising guaranteed returns.

## Rules

- Do not guarantee APY, liquidity, approvals, or token price appreciation.
- Label all yield figures as current, historical, simulated, or unavailable.
- Separate operational benefits from investment language.
- Escalate legal/compliance review for public claims about staking, lending, RWA collateral, or investor returns.

## Checklist

- Trust Score source: on-chain history, verified invoices, payment behavior, audits, KYC/KYB.
- DeFi utility: gas credits, collateral, escrow, liquidity pool access, treasury automation.
- Risk: smart contract, oracle, liquidity, regulatory, counterparty, custody, market volatility.
- Recommendation: allowed claim, needs evidence, needs legal approval, or reject.
