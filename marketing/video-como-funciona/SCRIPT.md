## Full video (EN)

### BeZhas

**0:00 — Your company is an island. BeZhas is the common port.**

> Your company is an island. Suppliers, banks, customs and customers each work with their own systems, their own paperwork and their own timelines. BeZhas is the common port. I'm the head of marketing at BeZhas, and over the next few minutes I'll show you how the whole platform works: every app, every plan, and what each industry and each role in your company gets out of it.

### The platform

**0:24 — Four layers, one account**

> BeZhas is built on four layers. At the bottom, the blockchain: BEZ-Coin, our ERC-20 token on Polygon, plus the contracts for payments, escrow, tokenization and governance. On top of that, artificial intelligence: OpenClaw coordinates multiple models, and an MCP connector lets your own AI work with BeZhas. Next come the SubApps, one for each business need. And at the top, OPERANT: AI agents that work department by department. All with one account and one invoice.

### Getting started

**0:58 — From zero to operating in five steps**

> Getting started is simple. One: sign up your company and verify your identity. Two: choose a plan. Three: the Hub and the Wallet come with every subscription. Four: turn on only the SubApps you need. And five: connect BeZhas to your systems through the API or SDK, or straight to your AI assistant through the MCP connector.

### Plans

**1:23 — A plan for every stage**

> There are four plans. Starter is free: a fifteen-day trial, and after that you only pay for what you use. Creator Pro, ninety-nine euros a month, for SMEs and creators: real-time data and your first crypto transactions. Business, four hundred and ninety-nine euros: advanced reasoning, market history, a managed ERP and zero data retention. And Enterprise VIP, two thousand four hundred and ninety-nine euros, for holdings and institutions, with parallel agents, a dedicated node and euro-to-euro transfers. Pay with BEZ-Coin and you save twenty percent, and on a yearly plan you get two months free.

**2:05 — What really changes when you upgrade**

> And it's not just a usage counter. Each plan opens money rails with clear ceilings. Above a set amount, a person has to sign off, and above a higher one, two different people. So your team moves fast day to day, and management stays in control of the big transactions.

### SubApps

**2:25 — BeZhas-Hub**

> Let's start the tour of the SubApps. BeZhas-Hub is the front door: your company's verified profile, a B2B services marketplace and the ecosystem's community. Here you find partners who have already been verified, instead of starting from scratch with every new supplier.

**2:43 — BEZ Wallet**

> The BEZ Wallet is included too. It's a non-custodial wallet: the keys belong to your company, not to us. The company can sponsor gas so employees don't need crypto to operate, and if someone loses access, the guardians you appoint can restore it.

**3:00 — BeZhas Pay**

> BeZhas Pay is your cash register. You create hosted payment pages, handle refunds and, if you sell online, connect it with a WordPress plugin. Your customer pays by card, by SEPA transfer or with BEZ-Coin, and settlement is recorded on Polygon.

**3:18 — BeZhas-DeFi**

> BeZhas-DeFi is your company's Web3 treasury: BEZ staking and farming, a bridge between networks, liquidity, and the DAO, where companies vote on network upgrades. And from the API, the SDK or your AI you can swap BEZ without leaving your own systems. Infrastructure spend becomes an asset that takes part in the network.

**3:42 — BZ PureScan**

> BZ PureScan gives your supply chain eyes. Computer vision scans products and documents, the Food Oracle checks food quality, and every asset can have an immutable digital twin. When someone asks where a batch came from, the answer is already on-chain.

**3:59 — BEZ-Energy**

> BEZ-Energy tokenizes energy. Energy-saving certificates, carbon credits and ESG data become verifiable assets, and companies can trade energy with each other on a peer-to-peer market.

**4:13 — BZ-CargoLink**

> BZ-CargoLink brings logistics and customs on-chain. Every shipment has its own NFT with its full history, the payment sits in an escrow that releases only when delivery is confirmed, and customs clearance can be verified without chasing paperwork.

**4:29 — OPERANT**

> And OPERANT, autonomous business management. These are AI agents that work by department: sales, support, finance, legal and more. On Starter they prepare drafts; on Creator Pro they assist you; on Business they work autonomously; and on Enterprise VIP their autonomy is governed by the DAO. Every action is audited and anchored on the blockchain.

### Your AI + BeZhas

**4:54 — Your AI already knows how to use BeZhas**

> All of this also works from your AI assistant. Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or your own agent to the BeZhas MCP server and ask in plain language. It looks things up, summarizes and prepares transactions. But it never signs or moves funds on its own: a person always does that.

### By industry

**5:15 — Logistics and customs**

> Let's look at each industry. In logistics and customs, the shipment leaves with its NFT, PureScan verifies the cargo at origin, customs checks the history on-chain and, once delivery is confirmed, the escrow pays the carrier. No waiting for invoices or reconciliations. We recommend Business for the call volume and the crypto-to-euro rail.

**5:38 — Energy**

> In energy, production is recorded, saving certificates and carbon credits are issued, they're sold on the P2P market or retired, and the ESG report comes out verifiable for the auditor.

**5:51 — Agrifood**

> In agrifood, every batch has a digital twin and the Food Oracle checks its quality. The distributor scans it and sees the origin. And if there's a problem, only the affected batches are recalled. A cooperative can start on Creator Pro.

**6:07 — Real estate and real assets**

> In real estate and real assets, the asset is tokenized in the RWA Factory, a primary sale of fractions opens, and buyers sign from their own wallet. For holdings and asset managers we recommend Enterprise VIP, with euro-to-euro transfers and dual approval.

**6:25 — Fintech and commerce**

> In fintech and commerce, checkout takes card, SEPA or BEZ, settlement is instant and refunds reconcile themselves. Start on Creator Pro and move to Business to convert to euros.

**6:39 — Manufacturing**

> And in manufacturing, every machine and every batch has a digital twin, OPERANT agents handle purchasing and maintenance, and tokenized machinery can serve as collateral with a bank.

### By role

**6:51 — CEO**

> Now, by role. For the CEO, BeZhas brings a network of pre-verified partners, immutable due diligence that cuts mergers from months to days, and a vote in the DAO on the network's future.

**7:05 — CFO**

> For the CFO, real-time settlement, ceilings and approvals by amount for every transaction, and twenty percent off the subscription when paying with BEZ-Coin.

**7:16 — COO**

**7:26 — CTO**

**7:42 — Legal and Compliance**

> And for legal and compliance, a platform designed with MiCA, the Spanish tax agency, SEPA and DAC8 in mind, with identity verification, zero data retention from Business up, and one rule that's not negotiable: the AI never signs.
