BeZhas Scan: Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Project Overview

BeZhas Scan is a high-fidelity 3D scanning sub-app within the BeZhas ecosystem. It leverages cutting-edge technology like Gaussian Splatting and Edge AI to capture, analyze, and tokenize physical assets on the BeZhas blockchain.

Core Target Sectors

  • Logistics & Supply Chain: Millimeter-accurate damage assessment and immutable liability tracking.
  • Real Estate & Valuation: Professional-grade "Scan-to-BIM" for accurate market comparisons and renovation forecasting.
  • Rental & Asset Management: Automated quality verification for cars, properties, and luxury goods.
  • Medical & Healthcare: Private, Edge AI-powered wound analysis and medication documentation.

Key Features

  • Gaussian Splatting Capture: For photorealistic digital twins.
  • Blockchain Tokenization: Every scan creates an immutable geometric fingerprint on the BeZhas blockchain.
  • Generative UI: The interface adapts based on the active sector mode (e.g., specialized tools for Logistics vs. Medical).
  • Smart Contract Integration: Automated insurance claims based on detected asset deformation in logistics.

Proposed Screens for Design

  1. Unified Dashboard: A central hub showing recent scans, blockchain status, and sector-specific shortcuts (Logistics, Real Estate, etc.).
  2. Adaptive Capture Interface: A "Smart Viewfinder" that changes its overlay and guidance based on what's being scanned (e.g., a "Damage Detection" mode for packages).
  3. Logistics Asset Audit: A screen comparing a package at "Source" vs. "Destination," highlighting deformations and calculating value loss.
  4. Real Estate Market Intelligence: A view showing a 3D floor plan with real-time market data overlays and valuation.
  5. Medical Wallet & AI Diagnostic: A secure area for medical scans (wounds, prescriptions) with AI-driven insights and procurement suggestions.
  6. Blockchain Proof-of-State: A detailed view of a scan's tokenized data on the BeZhas ledger, showing the full chain of custody.

Design Aesthetic

  • Modern & Technical: A dark-themed, high-contrast UI (inspired by Polycam/Scaniverse) with "Neumorphic" tactile elements.
  • BeZhas Branding: Seamless integration with BeZhas design tokens for a unified ecosystem feel.
