## 1. Executive Summary

BeZhas is an enterprise SaaS/Web3 operational trust layer that connects existing business systems with verifiable digital evidence. The first commercial wedge is maritime, port logistics, customs workflows and intermodal handoffs, where multi-party operations still depend on fragmented emails, PDFs, spreadsheets and manual reconciliation.

The value proposition is not "crypto adoption". BeZhas helps operators certify operational milestones, reduce documentation friction and create auditable handoff records without forcing a rip-and-replace of existing ERP, TOS or back-office systems.

## 3. Product Architecture, Investor-Safe Version

BeZhas is built around four enterprise-facing components:

1. Edge Node: secure connector for ERP, API, IoT, webhook or dashboard events.
2. Aegis AI: operational audit layer that checks anomalies and compliance signals before registration.
3. Core Gateway: integration center for validation, wallets, payments, evidence and dashboards.
4. Sector smart contracts: modules for logistics, supply chain, finance, legal, energy, health, insurance and asset workflows.

Sensitive implementation details, internal addresses, RPC endpoints, deployment keys, internal services and security thresholds are intentionally excluded from this teaser.

## 5. Why BeZhas Can Matter

Maritime and port logistics have high-value coordination problems:

- many actors need to trust the same event;
- handoff evidence is fragmented across systems;
- disputes delay payments and operational closeout;
- audit and due diligence are slow because evidence is not shared by design.

BeZhas addresses that gap through a lightweight validation overlay:

- start with one flow, one corridor or one handoff;
- run parallel to existing systems;
- hash and verify evidence without publishing sensitive data;
- measure time saved, dispute reduction and reconciliation quality before expanding.
